# Financial Crime Investigator Track

## Overview
Master the skills needed to investigate financial crimes, corporate fraud, money laundering, and corruption. Learn techniques used by financial crime units, regulatory agencies, and investigative journalists to trace money through complex corporate structures and cryptocurrency networks.

## Skills Developed
- Corporate registry navigation (domestic and international)
- Beneficial ownership tracing through shell companies
- Cryptocurrency transaction analysis and wallet clustering
- OSINT techniques for financial intelligence
- Entity relationship mapping
- Financial document interpretation
- Sanctions and compliance screening

## Tools Mastered
- **Corporate Research**: OpenCorporates, SEC EDGAR, Companies House, ICIJ Database
- **Blockchain Analysis**: Chainalysis, Elliptic, Etherscan, GraphSense, Blockchain explorers
- **OSINT**: Maltego, Google dorking, Shodan, LinkedIn intelligence
- **Compliance**: OFAC SDN lists, World-Check, Refinitiv, FinCEN
- **Visualization**: Maltego, network graphing, timeline tools

## Modules

### Module 1: Corporate Intelligence
**Duration**: 15-20 hours  
**Campaign**: [[Shell-Corp-Investigation]]  
**Skills**: 
- Corporate registry navigation
- Ownership tracing
- Entity relationship mapping
- Personnel profiling

**Tools**: OpenCorporates, SEC EDGAR, Companies House, LinkedIn

**Outcomes**:
- Navigate international corporate registries
- Trace beneficial ownership through complex structures
- Identify shell company indicators
- Map corporate networks with 10+ entities

### Module 2: Cryptocurrency Investigation
**Duration**: 15-20 hours  
**Campaign**: [[Cryptocurrency-Tracing]]  
**Skills**:
- Blockchain analysis fundamentals
- Transaction tracing
- Wallet clustering techniques
- Exchange identification
- Mixing service detection

**Tools**: Chainalysis (demo), Etherscan, GraphSense, OXT.me, Blockchain.com

**Outcomes**:
- Read and interpret blockchain transactions
- Trace funds through multiple hops
- Identify exchange deposits/withdrawals
- Detect cryptocurrency mixers
- Build transaction flow visualizations

### Module 3: Advanced Integration
**Duration**: 15-20 hours  
**Campaigns**: Multiple investigations combining both disciplines  
**Skills**:
- Multi-INT fusion (corporate + crypto)
- International jurisdiction navigation
- Sanctions evasion detection
- Professional report writing

**Outcomes**:
- Conduct end-to-end financial crime investigation
- Trace money from shell company to crypto wallet
- Navigate multiple legal jurisdictions
- Produce publication-ready intelligence reports

## Learning Outcomes

By completing this path, you will be able to:
- Conduct professional financial crime investigations from start to finish
- Navigate corporate registries in 20+ countries
- Trace cryptocurrency transactions through multiple blockchain networks
- Identify money laundering patterns and shell company structures
- Map complex ownership networks with 50+ entities
- Screen for sanctions and regulatory violations
- Produce professional intelligence products for law enforcement or compliance
- Build evidence-based cases with documented methodology
